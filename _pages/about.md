I'm a retired executive, business owner/consultant, computer technician, programmer, and community volunteer in central Ohio, splitting my time between Champaign County and the Westerville/Columbus area.

My work blends hands-on troubleshooting with building practical solutions: Microsoft 365 automation, PowerShell scripting, Google Workspace integrations, Access/Excel tools, and C++/C#/Python development, as well as websites and digital resources for local groups. Away from the keyboard, I'm usually in the shop or on the farm: welding, woodworking, and bringing old tractors back to life.

I use this site to publish the guides, scripts, and write-ups I create for clients, community members, and my own projects. Most of it is practical and repeatable, aimed at solving a real problem.

## Community & Civic Work

I'm active in several Champaign County and central Ohio community efforts, including:

- creating and maintaining directories, forms, and online resources for civic groups
- building tools for organizations such as historical societies, Lions Clubs, and small nonprofits
- helping groups adopt modern digital workflows while keeping things simple and low-cost
- preparing documentation, letters, templates, and research that support transparency and community involvement, such as my [timeline of the Thor Equities data center proposal in Urbana]({% post_url 2026-10-02-urbana-thor-data-center-timeline %})
