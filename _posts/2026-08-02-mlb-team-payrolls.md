## Notes

- Figures reflect competitive balance tax (CBT) payroll, which includes average annual contract values plus benefits, not straight cash salary.
- **Div. Standing** is the team's rank within its own division (1st–5th); **Record** is current wins-losses.
- The 2026 luxury tax threshold is $244,000,000. Teams above it owe a tax bill on the overage. **Space to Threshold** is negative for teams already over it.
- **Tax Status**: "Non-Tax" teams are under the threshold; "2nd-Time" and "3+-Time" indicate consecutive years over the threshold, which raises the tax rate.
- **Active Salaries / Retained / Minor League / Pre-Arb / Arb Projected** break the tax payroll down by source: active roster salaries, money owed to players retained after a trade, minor-league salaries, and projected pre-arbitration/arbitration salaries.
- **ROIC Rank** ranks all 30 teams by tax payroll divided by wins (lowest cost-per-win = rank 1 = most efficient). Miami, Tampa Bay, and Cleveland lead in efficiency; the Mets, Blue Jays, and Dodgers trail.
- Standings and records reflect the current point in the 2026 season, ahead of the August 3 trade deadline.
- Source: Spotrac's 2026 CBT tracker and current MLB standings.
