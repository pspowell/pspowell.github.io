A juicy, buttery double-crust peach pie with a hint of cinnamon and lemon. Serves 8.

## Ingredients

### Crust

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| All-purpose flour | 2 1/2 cups |
| Granulated sugar | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Unsalted butter, cold, cubed | 1 cup |
| Ice water | 1/2 cup |

### Filling

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fresh ripe peaches, peeled and sliced | 6 |
| Granulated sugar | 3/4 cup |
| Cornstarch | 3 tbsp |
| Lemon juice | 1 tbsp |
| Ground cinnamon | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1/4 tsp |

### Topping

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Large egg (for egg wash) | 1 |
| Coarse sugar | 1 tbsp |

## Instructions

1. **Make the dough.** Whisk together flour, sugar, and salt. Cut in cold butter until pea-sized crumbs form. Add ice water a little at a time, mixing until dough just comes together. Divide in half, flatten into disks, wrap, and chill.
2. **Chill dough.** Refrigerate the disks for 1 hour, until firm.
3. **Make the filling.** Toss sliced peaches with sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, cinnamon, and salt. Let sit ~10 minutes while you roll out the dough.
4. **Roll bottom crust.** Roll one dough disk into a 12-inch circle and fit into a 9-inch pie pan, leaving overhang.
5. **Fill the pie.** Pour the peach filling into the crust, mounding slightly in the center.
6. **Top the pie.** Roll out the second disk and place over the filling, or cut into strips for a lattice. Trim, crimp edges, and cut vents if using a full top crust.
7. **Chill assembled pie.** Refrigerate for 15 minutes to firm up the crust before baking.
8. **Egg wash and bake.** Preheat oven to 425°F. Brush crust with beaten egg and sprinkle with coarse sugar. Bake 20 minutes at 425°F, then reduce to 375°F and bake ~45 more minutes, until golden and bubbling.
9. **Cool before slicing.** Let the pie cool completely (about 3 hours) on a rack so the filling sets before slicing.

## Notes

- Toss peaches with cornstarch right before filling to avoid a soggy bottom.
- If peaches are very juicy, add an extra tablespoon of cornstarch.
- A splash of almond extract is a nice twist.
