A creamy, nutty Japanese roasted sesame dressing inspired by the classic Kewpie version. Easy to make at home in minutes with simple ingredients.

| Total Time | Course | Cuisine | Servings | Calories |
|------------|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| 5 min | Sauces | Japanese | 32 (1 serving = 2 Tbsp) | 135 kcal |

## Equipment

- Mixing bowl
- Whisk

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Kewpie mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup toasted sesame seeds
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/2 cup maple syrup
- 1/4 cup applle cider vinegar
- 4 tsp sesame oil
- 3 Tbsp tahini *(optional)*

## Instructions

1. In a medium mixing bowl, add the Kewpie mayonnaise, toasted sesame seeds, soy sauce, maple syrup, apple cider vinegar, sesame oil, and tahini (if using).
2. Whisk (or use a fork) until all ingredients are combined and the sauce is smooth.
3. Let sit 10–15 minutes before serving so the flavors develop.

## Notes

- **Kewpie mayonnaise:** Regular mayo works, but some flavor is lost.
- **Tahini:** Optional, but recommended for a stronger sesame flavor.
- **Maple syrup:** Sugar or honey can be substituted.
- **Apple cider vinegar:** Rice vinegar or white wine vinegar can be substituted.
- **Toasted sesame seeds:** Available at most grocery stores, or toast your own in a dry skillet in a few minutes.
- **Use as a marinade:** Works well with chicken, pork, or salmon. Coat the meat and refrigerate 30 minutes to 1 hour, then grill or bake until fully cooked. Discard any sauce that touched raw meat.
- **Other uses:** Dipping sauce, or drizzle on salads, noodles, and rice bowls.
- **Storage:** Airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week. Shake or stir before serving.
